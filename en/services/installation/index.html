AC installation in Madrid

A good installation starts with the room, not a sales pitch. Before you choose a split system, check how the space is used, how much sun it gets and where the outdoor unit could legally and safely go.

What you should know first.

Information worth checking before agreeing to the work.

Choose the right size

Floor area alone will not tell you which system you need. Ceiling height, glazing, insulation and the number of people using the room all affect the cooling load. Oversizing a unit can also undermine comfort.

If you have several rooms, compare separate units with a multi-split configuration. The decision should be based on how many rooms you need to cool at the same time.

Check the building first

An outdoor unit may need permission from the homeowners' association and may face planning restrictions. Check these before confirming a location or purchasing the unit.

Drainage, electrical supply, the refrigerant pipe run and future maintenance access all belong in the conversation before a final quotation is agreed.

Compare itemised quotations

Ask whether the AC unit, installation labour, basic materials, longer pipe runs, access equipment and VAT are included. Different providers may quote different scopes of work.

Have a question?

Start with the practical questions.

Does a split AC installation price include the unit?+

No. The illustrative installation amount here covers labour and basic materials; the actual AC equipment is excluded and must be quoted separately.

Can I put an outdoor unit on the front of my apartment building?+

Not automatically. Community rules and relevant local requirements must be checked before confirming a location or buying equipment.

Why is my air conditioner running but not cooling?+

Check the cooling setting and accessible filters safely. If it still does not work, a qualified technician should diagnose the fault rather than assuming refrigerant needs topping up.

How often should I service my air conditioner?+

Follow the manufacturer's guidance and the applicable technical requirements. Frequency depends on how frequently and where the system is used.
