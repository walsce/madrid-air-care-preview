AC maintenance in Madrid

An air conditioner can run quietly for months and still need attention. Filters, drainage and performance deserve a check, especially when the unit is used heavily.

What you should know first.

Information worth checking before agreeing to the work.

What can be done at home?

Following the manual, you can check accessible filters and keep an eye on unusual odours, noises or water. Disconnect the equipment before routine filter cleaning.

Do not dismantle electrical parts or tamper with refrigerant pipes. Some work must be carried out by an appropriately qualified company.

How often is servicing needed?

There is no universal interval for every installation. Usage, dust exposure, equipment type, manufacturer's instructions and applicable rules determine the sensible schedule.

A shop running air conditioning daily may require a different maintenance plan from a bedroom that is only cooled occasionally.

What is included in a service?

Ask exactly which checks and cleaning operations are included. Confirm whether travel is charged and whether parts or further diagnostic work cost extra.

Have a question?

Start with the practical questions.

Does a split AC installation price include the unit?+

No. The illustrative installation amount here covers labour and basic materials; the actual AC equipment is excluded and must be quoted separately.

Can I put an outdoor unit on the front of my apartment building?+

Not automatically. Community rules and relevant local requirements must be checked before confirming a location or buying equipment.

Why is my air conditioner running but not cooling?+

Check the cooling setting and accessible filters safely. If it still does not work, a qualified technician should diagnose the fault rather than assuming refrigerant needs topping up.

How often should I service my air conditioner?+

Follow the manufacturer's guidance and the applicable technical requirements. Frequency depends on how frequently and where the system is used.
