AC repair in Madrid

If your air conditioner has stopped cooling, leaks water or makes a new noise, the important thing is finding out why. A costly replacement is not always necessary, but neither should you authorise a repair without a diagnosis.

What you should know first.

Information worth checking before agreeing to the work.

Start with safe checks

Make sure the remote is set to cooling mode and that the accessible filters are clean, following the manufacturer's instructions. Switch the unit off first. Do not open electrical components or the refrigerant system.

If you find water near electrics, a burning smell or another obvious safety risk, stop using the equipment and contact a qualified professional.

Understand the fault

Poor cooling can have several causes, from airflow restrictions to component failure. Adding refrigerant without proper checks is not an adequate diagnosis.

A technician should explain what has been established, what remains uncertain and what the repair will involve before the customer accepts the price.

Repair or replace?

Age matters, but it is not the only factor. Compare the component cost, energy consumption, parts availability and previous failures.

A clearly itemised repair quotation helps you decide without feeling pressured into buying a new unit.

Have a question?

Start with the practical questions.

Does a split AC installation price include the unit?+

No. The illustrative installation amount here covers labour and basic materials; the actual AC equipment is excluded and must be quoted separately.

Can I put an outdoor unit on the front of my apartment building?+

Not automatically. Community rules and relevant local requirements must be checked before confirming a location or buying equipment.

Why is my air conditioner running but not cooling?+

Check the cooling setting and accessible filters safely. If it still does not work, a qualified technician should diagnose the fault rather than assuming refrigerant needs topping up.

How often should I service my air conditioner?+

Follow the manufacturer's guidance and the applicable technical requirements. Frequency depends on how frequently and where the system is used.
